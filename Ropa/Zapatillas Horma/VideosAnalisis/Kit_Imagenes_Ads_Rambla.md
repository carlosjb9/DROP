# Kit de 7 creatividades estáticas · Rambla (Navarros) · Nano Banana Pro

**Paleta confirmada:** base blanco `FFFFFF` (y off-white `F6F4EF` donde haga falta textura), navy de marca `0D1529` para texto y fondos oscuros, **acento caramelo `C98A4B`** (el color de la suela, común a los diez modelos: así el kit sirve para cualquier color de Rambla) y acento secundario el color de la franja del modelo elegido (blanco/rojo → `D0202A`). Modo mezcla: 4 light + 3 dark (navy). Idioma incrustado: castellano de España, de tú.
**Referencia que adjuntas en cada generación:** la foto del proveedor del color que uses (por defecto Blanco/Rojo) como THE PRODUCT; en los prompts 2, 6 y 7 también la imagen base de Marta o Javi si aparece el pie/persona.
**Regla de honestidad del kit:** los testimonios van como `[RESEÑA REAL]`: se pegan reseñas reales con consentimiento (nombre abreviado, talla pedida). No se inventan personas ni cifras. Sin botones de marca dentro de la imagen; el CTA lo pone Meta.

## Prompt 1 — Tu problema vs nuestra solución (tipografía dramática) · LIGHT

```
Create a high-converting wide-fit barefoot sneaker ad using a "your problem vs our solution" layout with dramatic typography.

FORMAT:
Vertical 4:5

BACKGROUND:
Soft off-white (#F6F4EF), very clean, subtle paper-like grain, no gradient.

---

TOP SECTION — DRAMATIC HEADLINES:

LEFT HALF (top), two lines stacked, left-aligned, generous space at the top:
Line 1: "tu" — lowercase, bold italic sans-serif, navy (#0D1529), medium-large.
Line 2: "problema" — lowercase, heavy bold sans-serif (NOT italic), caramel (#C98A4B), larger, dominant.

RIGHT HALF (top), mirroring the left:
Line 1: "nuestra" — lowercase, bold italic sans-serif, navy.
Line 2: "solución" — lowercase, heavy bold sans-serif, navy, larger.
A HAND-DRAWN caramel (#C98A4B) circle scribbled around the word "solución", marker-on-paper feel, slightly wobbly, line overlapping itself imperfectly at the ends.

---

MIDDLE SECTION — LEFT HALF: PROBLEM BUBBLES

5 iMessage-style received bubbles (light gray fill #E5E5EA, navy text, tail pointing LEFT) stacked down the left half, each at a slightly different horizontal position and spacing, slight rotation ±1-2 degrees, lowercase, no emojis:

Bubble 1: "me quito las zapatillas nada más entrar por casa"
Bubble 2: "pido una talla más y me sobra medio dedo por delante"
Bubble 3: "los dedos se me quedan en punta al descalzarme"
Bubble 4: "las anchas que encuentro parecen de señora"
Bubble 5: "en mi talla hay dos modelos y los dos aprietan"

---

MIDDLE SECTION — RIGHT HALF: PRODUCT (hero shot)

Place THE PRODUCT (provided as reference image) centered in the right half, at the same vertical level as the bubble cluster.
- Large scale, dominant on the right side
- Slight rotation (2-3 degrees), toe pointing slightly toward the viewer so the wide rounded toe box reads
- Realistic soft drop shadow grounding it on the off-white background
- Do NOT redesign or restyle the product — keep it exactly as the reference

---

BOTTOM SECTION (minimal):
Small centered line at the very bottom, navy text:
"Del 36 al 49 · puntera con la forma de tu pie"
No stars, no numbers.

NO badge in this variation.

---

STYLE:
- Dramatic editorial typography ad; chaotic problem side vs calm solution side
- Bold but premium; the hand-drawn circle and the caramel accent are the signature
- Cohesive with the campaign through navy text + caramel accent + product

ANTI-AI RULES (CRITICAL):
- The caramel circle must look like a marker scribble, NOT a clean vector circle
- Bubbles NOT perfectly aligned; each at slightly different scale or position
- "tu" and "nuestra" clearly italic; "problema" and "solución" clearly straight bold
- Real font kerning, slight imperfections; NO over-bright colors; product shadow photo-real
- No brand CTA button, no price, no URL

GOAL:
Agitates the exact sentences the avatar says about their feet and positions the sneaker as the single calm answer. Cold traffic, problem-aware, Spain.
```
